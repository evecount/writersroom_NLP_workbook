SCENE 1: CENTRAL PERK
[Scene: Central Perk. Rachel, Monica, Phoebe, Joey, Chandler, and Ross are sitting on the couch drinking coffee. Ross is looking miserable.]

MONICA: There's nothing to tell! He's just some guy I work with!
JOEY: C'mon, you're going out with the guy! There's gotta be something wrong with him!
CHANDLER: All right Joey, be nice. So does he have a hump? A hump and a hairpiece?
PHOEBE: Wait, does he eat chalk?
MONICA: Just, 'cause, I don't want her to go through what I went through with Carl- oh!
ROSS: (entering) Hi.
JOEY: This guy says hello, I wanna kill myself.
MONICA: Are you okay, sweetie?
ROSS: I just feel like someone reached down my throat, grabbed my small intestine, pulled it out of my mouth and tied it around my neck.
CHANDLER: Cookie?
MONICA: (explaining to the gang) Carol moved her stuff out today.
JOEY: Ohh.
CHANDLER: (sarcastically) Could this day BE any more depressing?
PHOEBE: Give her a break, Ross. It's tough when a marriage ends.
ROSS: No, I don't want a break! We were on a break! Well, actually, that happens later in season three. Right now, I just want to be married again!
[Rachel enters in a wet wedding dress, looking frantically around the coffee shop.]
CHANDLER: And I just want a million dollars!
MONICA: Rachel?!
RACHEL: Oh God Monica, hi! Thank God! I went to your apartment, but you weren't there, and some guy with a big hammer told me you'd be here!
JOEY: (smiling charmingly at Rachel) Hey, how you doin'?
MONICA: Joey, stop hitting on her! She's hysterical!
RACHEL: I just realized I'm wearing a wedding dress and I didn't marry Barry!

SCENE 2: MONICA'S APARTMENT
[Scene: Monica and Rachel's Apartment. Ross, Chandler, and Joey are helping Ross assemble Scandinavian flat-pack furniture.]

ROSS: I'm supposed to attach bracket A to slot B, but this Allen wrench is completely stripped!
JOEY: Don't look at me, Ross. When I need furniture, I find a nice recliner on the curb.
CHANDLER: Could that Allen wrench BE any more useless? I mean honestly, who designs these Swedish torture devices?
ROSS: (holding up a wooden peg) What is this? This is an extra piece! I have leftover parts!
JOEY: Dude, it's a spare. Like an extra kidney.
ROSS: You don't have spare parts when building a velociraptor skeleton at the museum! Paleontology requires absolute anatomical fidelity!
CHANDLER: And living with you requires earplugs.

SCENE 3: THE STAIRWELL
[Scene: Ross, Chandler, and Rachel trying to carry a giant sofa up a narrow stairwell.]

ROSS: Okay, we're gonna lift and turn! Ready?
CHANDLER: I am ready to drop this sofa directly on my foot.
ROSS: Lift... and turn! Pivot! Pivot!
CHANDLER: Shut up! Shut up! Shut up!
ROSS: PIVOT!
CHANDLER: I don't think it can pivot any more without violating the laws of three-dimensional Euclidean physics!
ROSS: (groaning) It's wedged. It is permanently wedged.

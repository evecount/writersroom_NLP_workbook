SCENE 1: APARTMENT 4A - LIVING ROOM
[Scene: The apartment. Sheldon and Leonard are sitting on the couch eating takeout Thai food. Sheldon is meticulously positioning his utensils.]

LEONARD: Penny said she's having trouble with her car engine again. I thought I might go over and take a look.
SHELDON: Why? Are you suddenly credentialed in internal combustion mechanics, or is this merely another desperate attempt to exchange unreciprocated romantic affection for amateur vehicular repair?
LEONARD: It's just a loose fan belt, Sheldon.
SHELDON: Technically, Leonard, your probability of repairing that engine without inducing catastrophic mechanical failure is less than twelve percent.
HOWARD: (entering with Raj) Gentlemen! Feast your eyes on the latest piece of NASA waste management hardware!
RAJ: (whispering to Howard) Howard, please don't describe the zero-gravity toilet before we eat dinner.
SHELDON: Howard, an engineering feat is merely physics applied by individuals who lack the intellectual rigor to solve differential equations.
HOWARD: Hey! I have a Master's degree in engineering from MIT!
SHELDON: Yes, and my sister has a swimming certificate from the YMCA. Both are equally irrelevant in the realm of quantum electrodynamics.
LEONARD: Sheldon, be nice.
SHELDON: I am being nice! I didn't mention that his belt buckle is an aesthetic catastrophe. Bazinga!

SCENE 2: THE CALTECH CAFETERIA
[Scene: Cafeteria. Leonard, Sheldon, Howard, and Raj are eating lunch at their usual table.]

SHELDON: I have discovered a profound flaw in the string theory compactification calculations published by the Cambridge group.
LEONARD: Oh really? Did you account for the Calabi-Yau manifold curvature?
SHELDON: Of course I accounted for the manifold curvature! What do you take me for, an experimentalist?
RAJ: (nodding) He has a point, Leonard. You do play with lasers all day.
HOWARD: Lasers are cool! Lasers can burn a hole through a tungsten carbide plate!
SHELDON: Lasers are applied photonics. Children play with laser pointers in cinema auditoriums. Theoretical physics, on the other hand, decodes the fundamental fabric of spacetime.
LEONARD: Could you pass the mustard, Dr. Fabric-of-Spacetime?
SHELDON: Only if you acknowledge that the yellow mustard in question exhibits non-Newtonian fluid dynamics when subjected to shear stress.
PENNY: (walking over with a tray) Hey guys! What are we talking about?
SHELDON: We are discussing the thermodynamic viscosity of condiments.
PENNY: Cool, I have no idea what that means. Did you fix my car, Leonard?
SHELDON: He did not, Penny. Because Leonard's understanding of machinery is strictly theoretical, much like his dating life. Bazinga!

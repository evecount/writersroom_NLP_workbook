SCENE 1: MONK'S DINER
[Scene: Monk's Diner. Jerry and George are sitting in their usual booth examining a diner menu.]

JERRY: You know what I noticed about the ketchup bottle?
GEORGE: What about the ketchup bottle?
JERRY: Nobody ever hits the bottom. They tap the neck. Why do they tap the neck? Is there an aerodynamic advantage to tapping the neck?
GEORGE: Jerry, I have bigger problems than condiment aerodynamics! Susan wants me to look at wedding invitations! High-grade vellum with embossed calligraphy!
JERRY: Vellum? That sounds fancy.
GEORGE: It is fancy! And expensive! Why do we need vellum? Who is inspecting the paper quality of an invitation? You look at it, you see the date, you throw it in the recycling!
[Elaine walks in carrying a bag from the soup stand.]
ELAINE: You are not going to believe who I just saw on 55th street.
JERRY: The Soup Nazi?
ELAINE: Yes! The queue was around the block! I waited forty-five minutes for turkey chili!
GEORGE: Did you follow the ordering protocol? You have to step to the left! If you don't step to the left, he yells at you!
ELAINE: I stepped to the left! I had my exact change ready!
[Kramer slides into the booth abruptly with wide eyes.]
KRAMER: (gasping) Jerry! The air conditioning! It's gone!
JERRY: What do you mean it's gone?
KRAMER: The compressor blew! It's ninety-eight degrees in my living room! My butter is melting on the counter!
GEORGE: These pretzels are making me thirsty!
JERRY: Kramer, why do you have butter on your counter?
KRAMER: (gesturing wildly) Because room-temperature butter spreads evenly, Jerry! It's an artisanal culinary technique!
ELAINE: Kramer, you're eating melted salted butter with a spoon.
KRAMER: Giddyup!
